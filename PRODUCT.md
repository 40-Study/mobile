# 40Study

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
Students studying courses, lessons and quizzes. A separate parent experience exists.

## Product Purpose
Resume learning, see the study schedule, manage daily goals, and review progress.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Flutter with Bloc. Preserve existing workflows, repositories, navigation and Vietnamese content. The current redesign is explicitly limited to the student experience after login. Parent and authentication screens are outside scope.

## Brand Commitments
40Study. The user requests a restrained, flat interface: fewer rounded containers, no decorative gradients, fewer icons, and no nested cards. Implementation and design direction are delegated by the user's instruction to proceed.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing screens and assets in lib/features/student and assets/images. Some portfolio and instructor content is existing demonstration data, not verified product claims.
